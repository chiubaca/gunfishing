# Gunfishers

Gunfishers is a survival roguelike about catching living weapons, using them against monsters, and risking recovery of equipment lost by earlier characters.

## Language

**Gunfish**:
A fish that functions as a usable gun after it is caught. Gunfish species determine the weapon, such as a Pistol Gunfish or Peashooter Gunfish.
_Avoid_: Gun, weapon fish

**Run**:
A single timed survival attempt, beginning when a new character spawns and ending in success when the timer expires or in failure when that character dies.
_Avoid_: Life, session

**Persistent world**:
The map whose state carries across runs, including recoverable equipment dropped by dead characters.
_Avoid_: Run, level

**Threat**:
Visible pressure generated by fishing. Higher Threat attracts stronger monsters, tying the pursuit of better Gunfish directly to combat danger.
_Avoid_: Random encounter rate

**Fishing mode**:
The 3D top-down mode used while the character has their rod out.
_Avoid_: Combat view

**Combat mode**:
The third-person shooter mode entered when the character draws their equipped Gunfish.
_Avoid_: Fishing view

**Recovery cache**:
The equipped rod and Gunfish, with their upgrades intact, left in the persistent world after a failed run. Only the newest Recovery cache remains available.
_Avoid_: Corpse, grave

**Gunfish loadout**:
The two Gunfish equipped for combat during a run. Only the active Gunfish—the one held when the character dies—is preserved in the Recovery cache.
_Avoid_: Catch inventory

**Gunfish evolution**:
A change to a Gunfish's firing behavior or special ability created by merging duplicate Gunfish of its species. It is distinct from numerical stat upgrades bought with monster resources.
_Avoid_: Stat upgrade, rod evolution
